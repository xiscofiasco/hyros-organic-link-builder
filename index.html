el
this will apply a tag in Hyros; it's the minimum necessary for organic tracking (aside from the URL rules in Hyros)
htrafficsource
groups all tags of a specific type - think of it like a folder of tags
hcategory
an intermediate between the above two parameters if needed - think of this as a subfolder, when there are distinct families of tags that fall under the same traffic source

Why add a traffic source and a category? (htrafficsource and hcategory)

The EL parameter alone is sufficient. However, you could obtain the same effect by creating a URL rule directly in Hyros.

Adding the other two parameters lets you compare divisions and subdivisions of your traffic more easily. Here are three links that share one traffic source:

specific offer/purpose/placement
distinct categories
common source
?el=sept_offer_email
&hcategory=retargeting
&htrafficsource=email
?el=vsl_reminder_day2
&hcategory=nurture
&htrafficsource=email
?el=webinar_lastcall
&hcategory=webinar
&htrafficsource=email

You can have multiple EL parameters grouped under a category and multiple categories grouped under a traffic source.

All of these will be reflected in Hyros in the Performance Report accordingly:

htrafficsource=Traffic Source hcategory=Campaign EL=Adset

You can compare this hierarchy to that of the ads, in which multiple ads are grouped in adsets, which are then grouped in campaigns.
