Why add a traffic source and a category? (htrafficsource and hcategory)

The el parameter alone is sufficient. Adding the other two lets you compare groups, not just single links. Here are three emails that share one traffic source:

All three sit under one email source, so you can compare the performance of various categories of emails while still having them grouped together under the same traffic source. The el shows you exactly which email or offer achieved that.
